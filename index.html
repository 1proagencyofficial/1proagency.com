Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

The things founders usually ask before reaching out. Don't see yours? Message us on WhatsApp — we reply within a few hours.

Do I need to live in the US or UK to register a company with you?

No. Our formation service is built specifically for non-resident founders. We handle the US LLC or UK LTD paperwork, registered agent, EIN/UTR and bank account setup remotely — you don't need to visit either country.

How long does the full process take?

It depends on the service and how quickly documents come back from your side. On your free consultation, we'll give you a specific timeline for your exact project before any work starts.

How does payment work?

We ask for a 40% deposit to begin your project. Once the work is complete we walk you through it live — the remaining 60% is only due once you're satisfied, and full access is handed over right after.

What payment methods do you accept?

Safe & secure payment policy: we accept bank transfer (Bank Al Habib) and major crypto wallets. For security, we don't publish account or wallet details on the site — message us on WhatsApp or X and we'll send them directly, then just share a screenshot to confirm your payment.

Can I book a single service instead of the full bundle?

Yes. Every service is available on its own at the current 40% off pricing. The bundle is for founders who want all six handled together as one coordinated project at a lower combined price — it's entirely optional.

Which countries do you serve?

Both locally and worldwide. We work with founders across multiple countries and industries — wherever you're based, we can scope the right service or bundle for you.

What happens after I message you on WhatsApp or email?

We reply within a few hours with a short discovery conversation about your business, then send a clear plan, timeline and price for exactly what you need — no pressure, no obligation to continue.

Do you support the business after launch?

Yes. Registration includes ongoing compliance and annual filing support, and every build — store, automations or ad accounts — includes lifetime monitoring and optimization support after we go live together.

Do you collect client feedback?

Yes — after every project we ask for honest feedback, happy or not, so we can keep improving. If you've worked with us, we'd love to hear from you on WhatsApp or X.
